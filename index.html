About

Tech help that meets you where you are.

Boomer Tech Help is built around patience and plain language — whether you're picking up a new device for the first time or just want a few things explained clearly.

Older adults

For anyone who wants to feel more confident using a phone, tablet, or computer — without being rushed or talked down to.

Anyone feeling overwhelmed

If new apps, updates, or online accounts feel like they're changing faster than you can keep up with, you're in the right place.

Family members

Clear tutorials you can send to a parent, grandparent, or neighbor — so you don't have to explain it all yourself.

What You'll Learn

Practical skills, explained simply.

Every guide focuses on one real problem at a time — the things people actually run into, explained in plain language.

Spotting scams and suspicious messages

Learn the warning signs in emails, texts, and phone calls so you can trust your own judgment.

Using smartphones and tablets

Get comfortable with the everyday basics — from settings to texting to taking a photo.

Understanding email, apps, and accounts

Make sense of logins, inboxes, and the accounts behind the apps you use most.

Protecting passwords and personal information

Simple habits that keep your information safer, without needing to become a tech expert.

Solving common everyday tech problems

Learning useful features without the clutter

Discover the handful of features actually worth knowing — and skip the rest.
